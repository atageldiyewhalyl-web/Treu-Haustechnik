All 40 images in this folder and its `form` subfolder are licensed stock **photographs** from Magnific (Freepik stock catalogue), downloaded on 2026-10-10.

- Every search was run with `content_type: photo` and `ai_generated: excluded`.
- Every chosen item was re-checked individually on its detail record; the `aiGenerated` column below is the value the platform returned for that item.
- No image-generation, upscaling or editing tool was used. The only processing is a format/size conversion with macOS `sips` (JPEG quality 80, longest side 2400 px for hero images, 1600 px for all others). Nothing was cropped or retouched, with two exceptions. The six small photos in `form/` are cropped to their object and brightened evenly (one gain value per photo, 1.01 to 1.20) so their plain backdrops match. And `hero-installer-wide.jpg` is mirrored left to right, so the wooden wall is on the left behind the headline and the installer and heat pump are on the right. Its content is otherwise untouched.
- "Camera" is the make/model found in the EXIF data of the downloaded original, where the contributor left it in the file ("—" means the original carried no camera EXIF, not that it is not a photograph).
